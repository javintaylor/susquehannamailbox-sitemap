PRE-LAUNCH AUDIT — MATERNAL CARE TASK FORCE MEMBER SITE
Conducted 2026-09-04, before any public deployment
Version audited 1.0.0 -> remediated in 1.1.0

METHOD
Seven independent review lenses over the 125 seeded content rows and the
front-end code, followed by adversarial verification of every finding: each
claim was handed to a skeptic instructed to refute it, with blocker/high
findings independently checked twice (once for factual accuracy, once for
practical consequence before launch). 28 raw findings; 26 survived; 2 refuted.

Lenses: PHI/re-identification; neutrality and conflict of interest; factual
accuracy and citation honesty; personal data of named members; front-end
security under anonymous access; data integrity against real values; clinical
scope and legal exposure.

EXPOSURE AT TIME OF AUDIT
None. The web app had not been deployed. The content spreadsheet was shared
with no one. Every finding below was corrected before the site became
reachable.

SEVERITY COUNTS
Blocker 5 (three describing the same defect from different lenses)
High 13, Medium 7, Low 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCKERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
B1. Action item AI-009 could re-identify one patient. (Found independently by
    three lenses.) The row read "Follow up on induction cancellation and
    transport case with OB nurse navigator (details shared privately, not on
    this site)", owned by two named clinicians. Combined with three other
    published rows — the member row naming a residential SUD program for
    pregnant women, that program's single Elkton address in Resources, and the
    Problems row attributing the account to "residential SUD treatment
    provider" — the singular "case" narrowed to one identifiable person in a
    program that holds a handful of residents.
    FIXED: rewritten as a standing process item owned by the co-chairs, with
    no case reference and no clinician owners. The Problems row attribution was
    genericised and the episode detail removed.

B2. A Task Force member's not-yet-open midwifery practice was published in the
    resource directory, together with her affiliated nonprofit in formation.
    A practice with no address, hours, or eligibility is not referable, and
    pre-announcing a member's for-profit business breaches the neutrality rule.
    FIXED: Published set to FALSE; the contact routing through a named member
    removed.

B3. Formula injection into the audit log. doGet passed the anonymous, unbounded
    ?page= URL parameter straight into the audit record. appendRow uses
    setValues semantics, so a value beginning with = + - @ is stored as a live
    formula — meaning an anonymous visitor could write executable content into
    the security log.
    FIXED: the parameter is allowlisted against known view names at the entry
    point, and auditSafe_ now coerces, control-strips, apostrophe-prefixes and
    truncates every audit field. An audit log must never execute what it
    records.

B4. Unverified adverse claims about a named hospital carried Confidence High.
    The ChristianaCare row asserted that scheduled inductions "are requests,
    not fixed appointments" and that Maryland Medicaid is accepted "for the
    time being", citing the hospital's own marketing page as source.
    FIXED: the induction claim removed from the directory row (it survives as a
    hedged, attributed statement in Problems), eligibility rewritten to direct
    readers to confirm coverage with the hospital, Confidence lowered to Medium.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
HIGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
H1.  Barrier log published the reporting organization beside 500 characters of
     narrative. Month + county + service + a single-site organization can
     re-identify a family. FIXED: org removed from the served payload and from
     the rendered card.
H2.  Member rows carried service advertising in role fields while other members
     got a bare line. FIXED: role fields reduced to professional identity.
H3.  "Delaware MFM practices do not accept Maryland Medicaid" stated as flat
     fact in two places with no source. FIXED: hedged and attributed in both,
     flagged as an open verification item for the Insurance team.
H4.  Voices of Hope recovery house rated High while the research report listed
     it as unverified. FIXED: rewritten to state what is actually known;
     Confidence lowered to Low.
H5.  Dr. Karen Blakemore, not a member, named publicly as a recruitment target
     in an action item and a meeting summary. FIXED: de-identified to role and
     institution in both.
H6.  The ShareContact gate covers only the Members tab; members named in
     free text elsewhere are not protected. DOCUMENTED as a review rule; the
     recruitment de-identification removes the live instances.
H7.  The entire Config tab was serialized to every browser with no gate (it has
     no Published column and cannot have one). FIXED: an explicit allowlist —
     only SITE_TITLE, TAGLINE and NEXT_MEETING are served; form URLs are read
     server-side.
H8.  Unreviewed barrier free text could reach the page as a chart label,
     bypassing co-chair review, because the month bucket was raw input.
     FIXED: month is parsed to strict YYYY-MM, and category/county/service are
     matched against closed vocabularies; anything else becomes "Unspecified".
H9.  The Data tab cited a 2024 Vital Statistics report that does not exist —
     VSA's latest published reports are 2023. FIXED: provenance restated as the
     Task Force slide deck citing 2024 VSA figures.
H10. Three of four Action Teams rendered with zero members, because the roster
     join matched on the team name's first word, so "Regional" swallowed
     everyone. FIXED: exact normalized token matching, verified in a browser.
     Empty rosters now reflect reality — only the Regional System of Care team
     has met.
H11. No publisher disclosure, official-status disclaimer, medical-advice
     disclaimer, or emergency guidance on a page branded with two county names.
     FIXED: footer carries publisher disclosure and official-status wording.
     NOTE 2026-09-04: the emergency/hotline paragraph was subsequently
     removed at the owner's direction because the site is a member working
     reference, not a public-facing service; language addressed to families
     or the public is out of scope. The footer keeps the member-facing
     no-PHI rule and the not-an-official-publication statement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MEDIUM AND LOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
M1. The served bundle was a denylist superset of what the page renders, so any
    new sheet column would ship silently. PARTIALLY ADDRESSED via the Config
    allowlist; a per-tab field allowlist remains open work.
M2. Confidence inflated to High on member-led organizations' unverified claims
    about themselves. FIXED, and a standing rule added: a fact sourced only to
    a member's report about that member's own organization is never High.
M3. Upper Bay rated High while its own text said the service was in
    development. FIXED: Medium, future tense, call-first caveat.
M4. DateVerified stamped on rows whose only source was a member report, so the
    page printed "Verified <date>" for something never checked. FIXED: those
    rows carry no DateVerified and the client renders "Reported by a Task Force
    member — not independently verified".
M5. The Members tab has no provenance column while every other content tab has
    one. DOCUMENTED: unconfirmed credentials removed pending confirmation; a
    consent note added; adding SourceOfRole/DateVerified remains open work.
M6. readPublished_ failed OPEN when the Published header was missing or
    renamed, publishing an entire tab. FIXED: fails closed, returns nothing,
    logs an audit row naming the tab.
M7. The "Both" county filter hid all statewide and national rows. FIXED.
L1. A non-URL SourceURL rendered as a dead "Source" label. FIXED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
REFUTED (reported, then dropped on verification)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two findings did not survive. One duplicated B1 from a weaker angle. One
claimed the workforce problem statement was written to point at a member's
future practice; reading the row showed it describes a regional shortage and
mentions the practice only as a fact about capacity.
