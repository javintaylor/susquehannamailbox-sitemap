MCTF CONTENT SPREADSHEET — TAB AND COLUMN REFERENCE
Row 1 of every tab is the header. Column order does not matter (rows are
read by header name) but header text must match exactly. A column named
"Published" gates what the site shows; TRUE/yes/y/1 publish the row.

Config           Key | Value | Notes
  Keys used by the site: SITE_TITLE, SUBTITLE, TAGLINE, CONTACT_EMAIL, NEXT_MEETING,
  FIND_HELP_URL, BARRIER_FORM_URL, RESOURCE_FORM_URL.

Purpose          Order | Section | Heading | Body | Published
  Section groups paragraphs into bands (Purpose, Who we serve, How we work).

Problems         Order | Title | Description | Counties | RelatedActionTeam |
                 TargetIndicators | SourceOrEvidence | Published
  Counties: Cecil, Harford, Both. TargetIndicators: semicolon-separated
  names from the Data tab (the six indicators) or "Cross-cutting".

Structure        Order | Level | Name | Purpose | CoChairs | StaffSupport |
                 MeetingCadence | TargetIndicators | Status | Published
  Level: "Steering Committee" or "Action Team".

Members          Order | Name | Credentials | Organization | RoleTitle |
                 County | ActionTeams | SteeringCommittee | Email | Phone |
                 ShareContact | Status | InternalNotes | Published
  ActionTeams: comma-separated team names (matched on first word).
  Email/Phone are served ONLY when ShareContact = TRUE. InternalNotes never
  leaves the sheet.

Funding          Order | Name | Source | Level | Amount | Status |
                 Description | IntendedUse | Counties | Indicators | URL |
                 Published

Resources        Order | Name | Type | Category | Counties | Indicators |
                 Description |
                 Eligibility | Contact | URL | SourceURL | DateVerified |
                 Confidence | Published
  Type: Federal, State, Local, Data. Category: Funding, Clinical,
  Home Visiting, Behavioral Health, Transportation, Insurance, Data,
  Community, Nutrition, Substance Use, Hotline. Counties: Cecil, Harford,
  Both, Statewide, National. Contact must be a PUBLIC phone/email only.

Data             Order | Indicator | Definition | Cecil | Harford | Maryland |
                 Unit | Year | Source | SourceURL | Published
  Aggregate public statistics only. Never a cell count small enough to
  identify a person (follow the source's suppression rules).

Drivers          Order | Indicator | Driver | WhatTheEvidenceShows |
                 NationalData | MarylandData | LocalData | DataSystem |
                 ImplicationForAction | RelatedActionTeam | Source |
                 SourceURL | Confidence | Published
  One row per cause/risk factor per indicator. Shown on Drivers & Evidence.

CaseStudies      Order | Name | Location | Years | ProblemFaced | WhatTheyDid |
                 Result | EvidenceQuality | Funding | Transferability |
                 Indicators | SourceURL | Published
  Communities that measurably improved comparable numbers.

Meetings         Date | ActionTeam | Title | Summary | Decisions | NextSteps |
                 NotesURL | Published
  Summaries must not contain patient stories with identifying details.

ActionItems      ID | ActionTeam | Item | Owner | DueDate | Status |
                 Indicators | Created | Updated | Published
  Status: Open, In progress, Done. Do not delete; mark Done.

Announcements    Date | Title | Body | LinkURL | ExpiresOn | Published
  Rows past ExpiresOn are hidden automatically.

BarrierLog       (Google Form destination — do not edit header)
                 Timestamp | Email Address | Month of occurrence | County |
                 Barrier category | Service type | Reporting organization |
                 Brief description | May a co-chair contact you for more
                 detail? | Review status | Reviewed by | Published
  Email Address is the REPORTER, never a family. It is never served to the
  site. Free text is served only when Published = TRUE after review.
